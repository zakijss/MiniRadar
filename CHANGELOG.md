## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-02

### Fixed

- The on-screen keyboard now shows how to leave it: B on an empty field goes back, and the footer shows B Back, or MENU Cancel once there is text (MiniDeck 1.0.1).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-02

### Added

- The first release: a device scan with makers and open ports, Wake-on-LAN, and Settings to sort the devices (address, name or maker) and turn the port check off.
