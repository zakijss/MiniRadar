## [Unreleased]

## [1.0.2] - 2026-11-08

### Changed

- Wi-Fi off says to turn it on in the main menu's Settings.
- Unreadable saved devices say to fix devices.json before saving a device.
- Failed wake-ups, removals and saves say what to do next.
- An open port reads "Port 22 open".
- Grey and red small text is easier to read (MiniDeck 1.0.3).

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-02

### Fixed

- The on-screen keyboard now shows how to leave it: B on an empty field goes back, and the footer shows B Back, or MENU Cancel once there is text (MiniDeck 1.0.1).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-02

### Added

- The first release: a device scan with makers and open ports, Wake-on-LAN, and Settings to sort the devices (address, name or maker) and turn the port check off.
